# Offshore Hydrogen: Economic and Environmental Impacts

Project report for the **Ocean Resources** course, MSc in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering, Instituto Superior Técnico (Lisbon), academic year 2024/2025.

📄 **[Read the report (PDF)](Artigo_RO_Final.pdf)**

## Abstract

This report reviews offshore green hydrogen production from renewable marine sources, mainly offshore wind. It covers production technologies, infrastructure, storage and transport, and assesses the economic and environmental impacts of the concept, with case studies of Portugal, the North Sea, China and Brazil.

## Contents

- **Offshore hydrogen concept**: electrolysis (AEL vs PEM), desalination, biological and thermochemical routes, SMR with CCS
- **Infrastructure**: offshore hydrogen production platforms, storage (compressed, liquid, LOHC, salt caverns, underwater), transport (pipelines, ships, ammonia/methanol carriers)
- **Environmental impacts**: structural and logistical challenges, life cycle assessment (LCA), water use, brine discharge, critical materials, marine ecosystems, fisheries
- **Economic analysis**: CAPEX/OPEX, levelized cost of hydrogen (LCOH), delivery and storage costs, policy and financing mechanisms
- **Case studies**:
  - Portugal: hydrogen production at WindFloat Atlantic (small- vs large-scale scenarios), national initiatives, H2Med
  - North Sea: offshore energy hubs, curtailment and power prices
  - China: Rudong solar-hydrogen project, coastal provinces, supply to Japan
  - Brazil: offshore wind potential, Petrobras, impacts in Ceará
